Receipt Vault
Connect Claude
  1. In Claude, open Settings, then Connectors, then Add custom connector.
  2. Name it Receipt Vault and paste this link. Treat it like a password.
  3. In a chat, attach a receipt photo and say: "Add this to my Receipt Vault."
Making a new link disconnects the old one.
